How it works

Balanced day. Following the 2025–2030 Dietary Guidelines ("the New Pyramid"), a day needs protein foods, dairy, vegetables, fruits, healthy fats and whole grains. A source counts for a group only if it produces at least a month of that group for one person each year.

Trip. From the clicked point, the map finds the shortest errand loop that visits sources covering every group and returns home (one stop can cover several groups). It tries the 6 nearest sources of each group. Distances are straight-line × 1.3 to approximate roads. "Separate trips" adds up a round trip to the nearest source of each group. Trip score = 100 × ½^(km ÷ 50): 50 at 50 km, 25 at 100 km.

Supply. Food made within the radius (closer farms weighted more) is turned into person-days per group. Daily needs used: 100 g protein from protein foods, 735 g dairy (3 cups), 240 g vegetables, 240 g fruit, 85 g whole grains, 30 g fat from fat-rich foods. Each group's sufficiency = 1 − e^(−people fed ÷ target); the score averages the groups (the scarcest is flagged) and multiplies by a diversity factor from the effective number of foods by calories.

Data and estimates.

  • Crops: County AWM grower sites (2020). Mixed farms are split by calibrating to the 2017 County Crop Report acreage for each crop.
  • Meat, milk and eggs: County Crop Report totals (cattle, sheep, hogs, milk, eggs, chickens) spread over forage, pasture and egg sites. This is a rough placement.
  • Fishing: piers, landings, stocked lakes, a shellfish farm and open-coast shore points, with rough yearly catch potential.
  • Not modeled: seasons, prices, whether farms sell directly, backyard food, and imports.
